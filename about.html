About

I’ve been trying to build my own website for years. I’d learn HTML, C, and Python and get pretty decent at them, and then it would all fall apart as soon as I took a break and came back. I’ve known about AI since around 2023, but I didn’t pay it much attention because I didn’t think it’d be worth my time. Still, a small part of me got excited every time I read about how close we were getting to having our own C-3PO or J.A.R.V.I.S. on call for everyday stuff. What I didn’t see coming was that AI would fill in the gaps in my patchy computer science knowledge. In the last couple of weeks alone, I’ve done more than I ever had before. AI took a website I’d spent two months on, rebuilt it, and added new features in about 20 minutes. That’s impressive. I know AI gets a lot of flak for what it’s doing to the environment, but I think people tend to judge it unfairly. Plus, a lot of that blame belongs on the companies that build data centers in places where they drain the life out of the towns around them. smh.
