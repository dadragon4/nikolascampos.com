About

I’ve been learning and attempting to build my own website for years. I would learn HTML, C and Python and get pretty decent, all for it to fall apart when I take a break from it and come back. Since 2023, I’ve known about AI and its advancements yet didn’t pay too much attention to it as I thought it wouldn’t be worthwhile; still a small piece of me would be excited reading about how we were getting closer to having our own C3PO or J.A.R.V.I.S. ready at all times to help us with day to day tasks. What I didn’t know was that I would be combining both my love for tech and AI to help cover the pitfalls I would leap into with my broken knowledge of computer science. With AI I’ve been able to do so much more than I ever have been in the last couple of weeks. It took my website of 2 months work and rebuilt it and added more features in just 20 minutes–that’s impressive. I know the world gives AI a lot of flack for what it’s doing to the environment but I feel like we tend to shift towards an unfair perspective of what it does already; plus the blame should shift to the companies that poorly build the data centers in areas that drain the life out of the townsfolk, smh.
