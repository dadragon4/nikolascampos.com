About

I’m Nikolas Campos, an IT Support Specialist at Valencia College with a background in media production. For the past three years, I’ve supported the technology that faculty and students rely on every day, from classroom A/V systems and live streams to campus tech support. In that time, I’ve become a trusted source of support and guidance across the college, and the work has taught me patience, clear communication, and confidence. I’m currently working toward my Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certification as I grow into cloud technology.

My path began at Valencia, where I started my associate degree in 2019 before transferring to UCF in 2021. In 2024, I graduated with a degree in Media Production and Management and a minor in Music. As I finished my degree, I made a short documentary about Journey Church Orlando. That project connected me with people who strengthened my faith and helped lead me to where I am today.

I’ve been making music for more than ten years, since I was 12. That love has introduced me to new technology, new people, and new cultures, and along the way I picked up audio engineering as a hobby. Today I write, produce, and release my own music, and I write, shoot, and edit films like Waiting.

That combination is what I bring to every project: an IT professional’s patience paired with a creative’s eye and ear. Whether I’m running a live stream, building a website, or editing a video, I care about making technology work for the people who use it.
