About

Hi, I’m Nikolas Campos, but you can call me Niko. I love writing stories and songs, and I keep a running record of my thoughts. I’m also a big tech person, and I love putting it to work: shooting and editing videos, building websites with AI and deploying them. Technology has always been a huge part of my life (thanks, Pop). I learn a lot by watching how people move through the world and by reading whatever I can get my hands on. By day I work in IT. By night I’m writing songs and playing video games, mostly Halo, though I recently got back into Call of Duty. I love spending time with my wife, taking trips to Disney, and checking in on my friends. I’m still learning about myself and the world. The wider my horizons get, the scarier life can seem, but I’m grateful my foundation is built on faith in Jesus.
